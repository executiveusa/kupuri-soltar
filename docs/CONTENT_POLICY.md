# Content Policy — SOLTAR / SUELTA

## Bitácora Essays

- Long-form, editorial tone
- Written by practitioners, not influencers
- No sponsored content without clear labeling
- No trauma narratives used for engagement
- No before/after emotional arcs designed to manipulate

## Cartas de Soltar

- Anonymous by default
- No usernames, no profile photos, no follower counts
- No likes, shares, or comment threads
- Letters moderated for safety, not popularity
- Letters cannot be monetized individually

## User-Generated Reflections

- Stored locally on device by default
- Never transmitted without explicit opt-in
- Never used for training data
- Never shown to other users without consent

## Language

- Default: Spanish (Mexico)
- Secondary: English, Japanese
- Translation is conceptual, not literal
- Japanese language only when the concept is meaningfully introduced

## Prohibited Content Categories

- Promotion of any product or service inside the journey
- Weight loss, diet, or body modification framing
- Comparative wellness ("other women do X")
- Medical advice or therapeutic diagnosis
- Crisis exploitation
- Productivity or optimization framing applied to emotional processing
