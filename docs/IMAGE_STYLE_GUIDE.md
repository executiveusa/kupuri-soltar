# Image Style Guide — Estilo Soltar

## Palette

| Role | Color Family |
|---|---|
| Background base | Parchment (`#F5F0E8`), Beige (`#EDE8DC`) |
| Surfaces | Mineral beige, soft clay, candlelight |
| Accent | Candle amber (`#D4943A`), muted rose (`#C4917A`) |
| Text / ink | Charcoal ink (`#3A3530`) |
| Atmosphere | Mountain mist, linen texture, cherry blossom detail |

**Avoid:** neon, pure black, pure white, harsh saturation.

## Composition Rules

- One focal point per image
- Generous negative space
- Slow visual rhythm — nothing is competing for attention
- No visual shouting (no bold graphic shocks, no high-contrast clashing)

## People Guidelines

- Women of many races, always
- Calm posture
- Non-performative — no exaggerated emotion, no "wellness face"
- Dignity over drama
- No models holding products awkwardly

## Typography in Images

- Headers: editorial serif
- Body: calm readable sans
- High legibility, generous leading
- Do not use Japanese text as decoration — only when conceptually meaningful
- Do not force translated text

## Cultural Use

- Do not perform culture
- Do not use symbols as costume
- Japanese language only when the concept is being introduced meaningfully
- Translate conceptually, not literally

## Image Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Do Not |
|---|---|
| Warm, soft lighting | Harsh studio lighting |
| Natural textures (linen, paper, wood) | Sterile white backgrounds |
| Quiet moments of transition | Forced "empowerment" poses |
| One woman, one moment | Stock collage of multiple scenes |
| Folded fabric, a single book, a candle | Product flatlay grid |
| Cherry blossom as atmosphere | Cherry blossom as costume |
